When we want to make a suggestion or an invitation to someone which includes ourselves, we can use the expression _Let's_.

_Let's_ is the short form of "Let us". _Let us_ is very formal and not very common.

Examples:

-  _Let's_ get something to eat.
- Yes. _let's_ go to Charley's.

When you use _Let's_ you must use the verb in _simple form_.

Example:

_It's_ very hot today.

- Let’s _to get_ an ice cream – **INCORRECT**
- Let’s _getting_ an ice cream- **INCORRECT**
- Let’s _GET_ an ice cream – **CORRECT**
