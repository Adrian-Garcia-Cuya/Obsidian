### What is?
Adverbs of frequency are words that describe the frequency with which an activity is performed.

An adverb is a word that modifies and describes a verb, adjective, or group of words. Adverbs of frequency typically modify verbs, or action words, and show how often they occur. Modifies it by adding additional information to the verb.

### Forms
- *Before* a main verb (except with To be).
> ==Subject== + _adverb_ + main verb
> 
> 1. I _always_ remember to do my homework.
> 2. He _normally_ gets good marks in his exams.

 - *After* the verb To be.
 > ==Subject== + _adverb_ + main verb
 > 
 > 1. They are _never_ pleased to see me.
 > 2. She isn't usually bad tempered.
 
- We can also use the following adverbs at the start of a sentence.
> _usually_, _normally_, _often_, _frequently_, _sometimes_, _occasionally_
> 
> 1. _Occasionally_, I like to eat Thai food. 

- We *cannot* use the following at the beginning of a sentence 
> _always_, _seldom_, _rarely_, _hardly_, _ever_, _never_
> 
> 1. She _hardly ever_ comes to my parties.
> 2. They _never_ say 'thank you'.

- We use _hardly ever_ and _never_ with positive, not negative verbs.
> 1. She hardly ever comes to my parties.
> 2. They never say 'thank you'.

- We can also use the following expressions when we want to be more specific about the frequency.
> _every day_, _once a month_, _twice a year_, _four times a day_, _every other week_
